# EX-12: a causal edge before operational establishment

Registered before any new discovery or assay seeds are run. EX-11 remains closed
with unchanged results. Its two early sequences are exploratory motivation and
software fixtures only, never confirmatory EX-12 candidates.

## Hypothesis and unchanged substrate

An online-generated resource-processing capability B can make a later inherited
uptake change C beneficial before B completes EX-11's establishment criterion.
We distinguish functional dependency in standardized worlds from historical
opportunity in the actual birth-time ecological state. Historical necessity of B
is not tested in this experiment. Neither a deeper causal chain nor open-ended
evolution is claimed.

Use EX-11's exact Cell80 library, resource target, 64-input domain, 84-member
composition operator, mutation rates, energy accounting, dilution, replacement,
patch dynamics, 256 slots and 3,000-tick discovery horizon. Every founder is
primitive mask_xor/low uptake. No evolved genotype is seeded into discovery.
All 89 typed binary primitives must fail the resource certificate. A passing
program must be assembled online, and execution traps remain failures.

Four arms, ten paired discovery seeds per arm, 40 worlds total: full, B-only,
no-substrate, atomic-only. The fresh seed base is `0x5eedb4111e001000`, offsets
0 through 9. No seed search, adaptive stopping, added worlds, or rate/horizon
retuning after outcomes. Missing candidates are a valid inconclusive stopping
outcome. Controls and mechanical parameters are identical to EX-11.

## Candidate selection and temporal classification

Select the FIRST birth in each full world satisfying all of:

- parent belongs to a clean B lineage, whose origin is a newly capable low-uptake
  child of an incapable parent;
- all descendants on the path from that B origin to this parent retain the exact
  B program and continuously low uptake;
- child inherits that same program and toggles low to high uptake;
- the child's birth tick is later than B's birth tick.

Register the candidate online at birth, before any of its reproductive outcomes.
There is no establishment or success filter. Never substitute a later candidate.
Export the complete ancestry path and the actual pre-B parent genotype. A is
that parent's program with low uptake, not an arbitrarily chosen ancestral program.

Classify the candidate as pre-establishment if its clean B origin has NEVER yet
held >=20% of all 256 slots for 50 consecutive completed ticks, using EX-11's
existing rule. Report elapsed ticks since B, current capable fraction, current
clean-origin fraction, and the established/not-established flag before C's birth.
Pre-establishment is an operational label: it does not imply B is rare, that B
has not already spread substantially, or that a biological establishment threshold
exists. Later candidates are assayed but cannot support the primary temporal claim.

## Context 1: historical opportunity

Save a full snapshot immediately after the selected C child is inserted, before
the remaining births of that tick execute. Preserve every organism, slot, ID,
energy, genotype, nutrient stock, next-child ID, establishment counters and the
ordered list of pending historical parents. This includes the actual newborn's
energy (20), which is identical across this context's four arms.

Fork that snapshot into A, B, C-only and BC by changing only the selected newborn's
program/uptake and derived capability. Mark its descendants for measurement, but
do not otherwise alter competitors, resource stocks, energy, placement or age.
Disable further mutation in ALL organisms to isolate the focal genotype contrast;
competitors still interact, die and reproduce. Complete the saved pending birth
phase, then run 200 full ticks. Fitness is total births from the focal descendant
lineage, including direct births, over that continuation. Do not condition on
survival. Also record direct births and final descendants.

Ten fresh continuation seeds `base+200..209`, paired across the four genotypes.
The pending-parent ordering is retained; future random outcomes use the assigned
continuation seed. These are ten conditional futures of ONE historical context,
not ten independent evolutionary discoveries. Replication across discovery seeds
is reported separately. A mutation-off assay beginning at C's birth is not a claim
to measure instantaneous fitness or fitness under the completely unmodified future.

## Context 2: functional dependency

Ten separate fresh seeds `base+100..109`. Each standardized world starts with one
focal founder at slot0, energy24, among255 mask_xor/low competitors. Cross the actual
pre-B program versus B with low/high uptake (A, B, C-only, BC). Disable mutation in
all organisms and run 200 ticks, as in EX-11. Primary/secondary fitness measures
are identical to the historical context. Do not pool the two contexts.

## Fixed evidence gates

In EACH context separately compute paired deltas per assay seed:

`B benefit = F(B)-F(A)`
`C with B = F(BC)-F(B)`
`C without B = F(C)-F(A)`
`interaction = [F(BC)-F(B)]-[F(C)-F(A)]`

That context supports an edge only when B benefit >0 in >=8/10 seeds and in the
mean; C with B >0 in >=8/10 seeds and in the mean; C without B <=0 in >=8/10 seeds
and in the mean; interaction >0 in >=8/10 seeds and in the mean. These are fixed
screening gates, not calibrated p-values or a claim of universal necessity.
All seeds including extinctions and ties enter the denominator.

A candidate supports the primary nascent-edge claim only if BOTH contexts pass
and it was selected pre-establishment. Overall replicated success requires such
candidates in >=3 of the10 independent full discovery seeds. One or two passing
edges are reported as limited support, not overall success. No candidates means
dependency untested, not absent. Per-context outcomes remain visible even when
the overall gate fails. Shared assay seeds must not be pooled as independent data
across candidates.

## Restoration and replay checks

After removal arms in each context/assay seed, repeat BC from the identical initial
state. Its complete continuation hash, births and measured outputs must match.
Replay each candidate's full discovery world with ONLY that C uptake toggle
suppressed, verifying pre-birth state digest, preceding birth log, parent, tick
and unchanged program. Record later direct/descendant counts descriptively; this
is C's historical contribution, not B's historical necessity. Restore C by a
baseline replay and require the full original world hash/birth log to return.

Before accepting historical assays, resume the untouched snapshot with original
seed and mutation enabled. Require the entire remaining birth log, tick records,
and canonical final state to equal the original discovery. This validates exact
birth-phase continuation, not merely a similarly initialized world. Export the
snapshot and hash so its state can be audited independently.

## Discovery, retention and context-indexed edge records

Report separately, per arm, worlds with B discovery; establishment conditional
on discovery; final-200 capable fraction >=.5 plus establishment conditional on
discovery; and first-clean-C supply conditional on B discovery in the full arm.
Do not reuse EX-11's unconditional 8/10 B-only retention gate as an EX-12 gate.
The unchanged control checks remain: no substrate consumption when absent, no
capability/consumption in atomic-only, no high uptake in B-only, and at most2/10
no-substrate worlds with a clean B lineage established. All proportions retain
their denominators; zero opportunities is reported as untested/null.

Every selected edge records events, ancestry, actual pre-B background, contexts,
snapshot digest, intervention, paired effects, replication, candidate-selection
rule and evidence status. Historical necessity and environment mediation remain
untested. Only B→C edges are tested; no concatenation into multi-edge chains.
Report zero SUPPORTED edges when none pass but causal depth as unknown when
untested; do not interpret a failed screen as absence of any causal structure.
Candidate coverage is selected first-clean events tested / selected first-clean
events. It is not the fraction of all potentially causal pairs in the history.

## Scope and stopping

No variation-policy evolution, persistent construction, physics integration or
editorial work is included. Retain technical failures and document corrections
before affected reruns. EX-11 may be replayed only as a software equivalence check.
Final reporting separates confirmatory, descriptive and validation evidence.
