# EX-14: the dependency survived; realization was the bottleneck

Across 100 paired checkpoint futures, BC produced descendants in 64/100 worlds,
while B did so in 41/100. In the preregistered event subset where either B or BC
reproduced, `D = N_BC − N_B` was positive in 63/64 futures. The result is
therefore **realization-limited**, not evidence that the dependency eroded.

Unconditional realization remains primary: A and C-only realized zero births in
all100, B in41 and BC in64. The conditional analysis is a mechanistic decomposition
of paired counterfactual futures, not a replacement for unconditional fitness.
Zero/zero futures were retained in the realization denominator.

This is conditional on one transmitted opportunity and checkpoint ecology. It does
not show historical accumulation or a later D. Raw paired outcomes are in
