# CHRIS HAY

**A house for ideas, systems and objects.**

Building things to find out how they work.

## The identity

The person’s name is the house. `chrishayuk` remains the URL and handle. The
umbrella describes what the practice can contain; the philosophy describes how
Chris works. “A life in questions.” is the personal line beside the portrait.

This positioning, supplied by Chris on 6 September 2026, supersedes “A research
house” as the umbrella and “Technologist, Futurist and Disruptor” as the role line
on this site. Earlier production boards remain historical design references.

Ideas include research and the notebook. Systems include the software, models,
interfaces and infrastructure. Objects are the things made: software, films,
interfaces and publications. The umbrella allows future physical work without
claiming that any such object already exists.

LARQL, VINDEX3, HAUSE and MCP-CLI retain their own names, propositions and canonical
sites. Their local dossiers carry “A system by Chris Hay.” IBM productions remain
external appearances, with IBM credited as producer and Chris as panelist.

`lib/house.ts` holds the current author-described practice. About renders it and
the graph links to that source. Its retrieval basis is `author-description`;
this is not an experimental finding, a new legal organization, or a publication
of the draft research records.

## The catalogue architecture

Primary navigation now opens Ideas, Systems, Objects and Record, followed by About
and Ask. The homepage enters LARQL and VINDEX3 before the portrait and external
appearances. Its film scenes retain the shared motion owner and poster-first media.

- `/ideas`: open questions and notebook entries; no invented findings.
- `/systems`: the four existing dossiers, with stable `/work/:slug` URLs.
- `/objects`: authored films, the HAUSE interface study and publications.
- `/record`: server-rendered catalogue search, kind filters and pagination.

The legacy `/work` index uses the Systems presentation and canonical URL. Film,
Research and Notebook collections remain available under the house and footer.
The catalogue uses existing IDs rather than adding a decorative numbering scheme.
Release dates remain distinct from catalogue retrieval dates. Drafts are visible
in the catalogue but do not enter publication feeds or become evidence in Ask.

The graph connects the new entrances and all catalogue records. IBM films have
catalogue membership and participant credits, never house authorship. Highway
requires its actual programme source before a dossier or findings can be added.
