# CHRIS HAY

**A house for ideas, systems and objects.**

Building things to find out how they work.

## The identity

The person’s name is the house. `chrishayuk` remains the URL and handle. The
umbrella describes what the practice can contain; the philosophy describes how
Chris works. “A life in questions.” is the personal line beside the portrait.

This positioning, supplied by Chris on 6 September 2026, supersedes “A research
house” as the umbrella and “Technologist, Futurist and Disruptor” as the role line
on this site. Earlier production boards remain historical design references.

Ideas include research and the notebook. Systems include the software, models,
interfaces and infrastructure. Objects are the things made: software, films,
interfaces and publications. The umbrella allows future physical work without
claiming that any such object already exists.

LARQL, VINDEX3, HAUSE and MCP-CLI retain their own names, propositions and canonical
sites. Their local dossiers carry “A work by Chris Hay.” IBM productions remain
external appearances, with IBM credited as producer and Chris as panelist.

`lib/house.ts` holds the current author-described practice. About renders it and
the graph links to that source. Its retrieval basis is `author-description`;
this is not an experimental finding, a new legal organization, or a publication
of the draft research records.
