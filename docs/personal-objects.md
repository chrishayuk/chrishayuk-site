# Objects I return to

The personal objects entrance is `/objects`; the first lookbook is
`/objects/things-i-look-through`. This extends the existing Objects room while
retaining routes to made work, films and the notebook.

Chris supplied the personal accounts on 26 September 2026. Light sensitivity
is the framing: Swanwicks help indoors, sunglasses outside. Swanwick leads
with Chris’s actual pair from his films. The wider collection is permitted to
use generic reference pairs; each image is labelled and source-attributed,
with an explicit note that colours and finishes can differ from his own.
Juliet remains second, with Chris’s account of wearing it to formal occasions.
The other pairs have names only: no invented memories, acquisition dates,
model variants or medical claims.

The lookbook uses HAUSE EditorialPlate, the publication’s typefaces and its
existing motion coordinator. It is a photographic/editorial surface with no
lab paper or codex shell. The film still and muted excerpt are frozen copies
under `public/media/objects`, so a future latest-film update cannot silently
replace the pair in this lookbook. Both keep the original film overlay. Play
and pause respect the site’s motion and reduced-motion preferences.

`lib/personal-objects.ts` supplies visible copy, image credit and source links,
and the retrieval graph. Graph sources are labelled `personal-account`.
Possessions are collected by Chris; he is not credited as their manufacturer.
These pages have no scientific finding status and do not enter the notebook
publication feed.

Reference images were inspected and downloaded on 26 September 2026. They
remain unretouched and are not labelled as owned photography. Their source
URLs and SHA-256 digests are recorded in `content/objects-media.json`.
