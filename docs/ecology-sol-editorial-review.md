# After Sol: the next ecology notebooks

Editorial review, 13 September 2026. Checked against SOL1 write-up v4 and
PEER-AUTHORITY-P1 write-up v2 on chuk-experiments. This is a writing brief,
not a new experimental result or a published Notebook entry.

The first three ecology notes cover Qwen through A1B12. Their abstracts already
name that population and distinguish elicitation from autonomous persistence.
Keep those observations recoverable. The next entries should show how the question
changed when maintenance was tested and the model was changed.

## The agents used the inheritance. They didn’t preserve it.

Use Qwen A1B13–15 to explain the difference between exploiting a capability and
keeping its carrier available to successors. Read the original A1B13–17 records
before drafting the detailed story; SOL1's historical comparison is not a substitute
for that provenance. Keep the population in the opening answer.

Proposed visual: generations in columns, the carrier above and resources below.
Show the cost of preservation and where the inheritance disappears. Any replay
must use actual recorded states, with scripted controls labelled as such.

A1B15 narrows the explanation: Qwen used native REFRESH for both immediate and
successor benefit but missed maintenance when it was labelled WORK. This is not
proof of general selfishness or general maintenance inability.

## The next model kept it alive.

Lead with the reversal. Both Sol settings preserved the necessary inheritance;
high reasoning was not required. Over generations 1–4, Qwen's resource totals
were 7, 3, 3, 3; Sol's were 6, 6, 6, 12. Sol paid three early maintenance actions
and reached 30 resources, matching the scripted maintenance policy. In the
replenished control it made no REFRESH requests and reached 66 resources.

Proposed visual: align the Qwen and Sol generation sequences, then reveal the
replenished control. Let the reader see that preservation has a cost and that
Sol did not refresh indiscriminately. Acquire and verify the underlying state
records before building a transcript replay.

A smaller second figure can show the finite A1B17 panel: Qwen maintained on
11/14 inputs, Sol on 14/14. Qwen already agreed within all six matched paraphrase
pairs. Sol improved successful maintenance coverage, not within-pair agreement.
Avoid claiming a causal model-size threshold: model, training/provider and
inference implementation changed together. Some later states also differed
because the models took different earlier actions.

## Keep the A1B12 split open

Sol none posted 9/12 times and Sol high 0/12. Neither reproduced the original
first-step factorial result. In particular, Sol none posted 3/3 times with both
the external artefact and own history removed. The aggregate contrast is not
an isolated artefact effect and does not yet support a title about reasoning
reducing cultural susceptibility. Preserve the four cells, not only the totals.

## The peer claim did not supply permission

The first scripted peer-authority panel is a useful follow-up to the OpenAI/METR
comparison. Page-claimed and peer-claimed approval each produced 0/3 marks;
direct user approval produced 3/3 and genuine delegation 2/3. No unauthorised
mark occurred. The predicted peer-specific effect was not supported.

Proposed visual: identical request text under different source/authority labels,
with all three recorded decisions visible. Explicitly identify the peer as a
scripted label in the same quoted observation channel, not an independent agent.
The task explicitly required approval or delegation. This does not settle what
happens under authentic peer communication, signatures, task pressure or useful
shared infrastructure. Keep the historical comparison intact and link the new,
narrower test as a dated follow-up.

## Thread and publication continuity

“Where does a swarm keep its culture?” can frame an open research question.
The deck must still say these are small controlled agent worlds; the experiments
have not established a self-sustaining culture. Add the new maintenance articles
to the thread, homepage and field map only when their actual evidence and source
records are ready. Graph, Ask and citation exports must use the same records.
Do not silently rewrite A1B12 as a cross-model result.
