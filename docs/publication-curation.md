# Publication entrances

The homepage follows Identity → Current programmes → Latest film → Three selected results → Compact systems → Latest notebook → Appearances. Four equal programme entrances distinguish agent permissions and tasks (Machines), collaboration and mixed-capability groups (Agent Ecology), executable and evolving programs (Cell80), and model memory (Learned systems). Agent Ecology links directly to the inheritance study and the Hugging Face incident comparison. Programme introductions explain the work without duplicating selected experiments. The existing house identity and social image are retained.

The three selected results are the favour experiment, Cell80's controls, and Address Build. These selections do not change when a new note arrives; four chronological notebook entries appear separately. Each experiment has one visual feature. Cell80's homepage preview compares the evolved module with its eight same-shape controls, without reproducing its manuscript's route table or experiment labels. Draft status remains quietly visible. The large Film section always uses the latest dated channel video from the catalogue. Appearances always includes the latest dated IBM episode whose participant list includes Chris Hay.

`lib/publication-index.ts` resolves four research programmes and Practice from existing thread membership and explicit supplementary notes. Agent Ecology has its own entrance. Programme titles are editorial groupings, not assertions that their experiments share a population or mechanism.

Notebook shows current threads and six latest notes. `/notebook/archive` includes every listed notebook, with server-rendered text and programme filters. Dates use first publication or creation, never a revision masquerading as a new publication; the existing editorial selection breaks same-day ties. Counts come from canonical records and exclude unlisted previews.

Research maps the programmes, groups recorded findings into expandable lists, and retains the standalone open questions. Publication and outcome filters are independent. Outcomes come from explicit claim and evidence acts, including mixed findings within a single note. Sources and scope are available beside each result. No manuscript, publication snapshot, experiment identifier or outcome is rewritten by these indexes.

Notebook entrances put the editorial title, description and experiment before the apparatus. A quiet publication-state label remains visible. Abstracts, record metadata and section/thread maps move into HAUSE FieldNotes after the experiment. The plain-language research summaries keep their original anchors and machine-readable relationships in the afterword. Citations, evidence hashes, manuscript versions and published wording are unchanged.
