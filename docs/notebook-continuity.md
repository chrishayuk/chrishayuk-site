# Notebook material and exhibition essays

The homepage, notebook index, every collection drilldown and bound readers share
HAUSE material tokens for paper light, binding, shadows and the surrounding scene.
Collection previews keep perspective and wider spacing. Reading remains level,
with content-sized folios and the existing sticky modes and page controls.
Collection colours and the separate squared, unruled and dotted stocks persist.
Lighting affects paper backgrounds; photographs, videos and simulation pixels
retain their own values. Mobile previews are level and printing removes staging.

The exhibition-design article is deliberately different. It now uses HAUSE's
NotebookExhibition: a continuous visual essay, its existing visual study followed
by five authored rooms and a closing reflection. No simulated book or paper
surrounds those exhibits. The collection preview shows the credited visual study.
Existing act anchors, the first-entry anchor, room IDs and conclusion anchor are
preserved; the full manuscript and source apparatus remain reachable.

The rendering audit distinguishes exhibition rooms from lab folios and checks
their respective openings and conclusions while retaining the same manuscript,
citation-anchor, heading and duplicate-ID checks. Automated checks do not replace
visual browser review, which is unavailable in this session.
