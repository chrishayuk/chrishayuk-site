# Cinematic studies — 15 September 2026

Three original visual studies introduce a night-studio language to the current
reading experience: blue spill, amber object light, aluminium, glass and empty
space. They are metaphors, not photographs of the experimental world.

- **Threshold:** a message outside a closed door. Machines opening and Notebook entrance.
- **Inheritance:** a record remains beside an empty chair. Homepage research transition,
  Agent Ecology opening and Notebook entrance.
- **Descendant:** a mark repeats across glass plates. The pause before the corruption
  chapter in *The AI left. Its knowledge didn’t.*

Each appearance identifies the image as AI-generated and not experimental evidence,
including its alt text. Captions sit outside the image. No automatic animation,
new inference, new measurements or changes to preserved manuscripts are involved.
The existing social cover remains available; the homepage result preview now shows
recorded written renewal rather than repeating the cover's typography.

The two Notebook image entrances are staggered on desktop and stacked on small
screens. Other threads and latest notes remain typographic. Full-page studies use
a 16:9 frame on desktop and 4:3 on phones, with the subjects protected by focal
positions. Static images have dimensions, lazy loading and 800/1672-pixel sources.

The Ecology ending now asks about selective preservation and dependable correction,
reflecting the later routing-world results. Its fifth preview no longer reuses the
earlier autobiographical-memory illustration. The older key-world null remains in
its own step and manuscript.

Generation used the built-in image_gen tool. Exact prompts and original source
locations are in `visual-studies-prompts.json`; committed originals are in
`visual-studies-originals/`. `visual-studies-assets.json` records dimensions,
byte counts and SHA-256 hashes of originals and WebP derivatives. WebP conversion
only resizes/compresses the generated images; it does not alter their contents.

These assets are editorial artwork, not new experiment results. Canonical
experiment records and the published evidence bundles are unchanged.
