## Homepage cinematic treatment

The homepage applies a non-destructive CSS film grade to video and its matching poster: reduced saturation, slightly warm tones, deeper contrast, soft vignette and restrained edge shading. Mobile uses a lighter grade to retain visibility on small screens. The contact sheet alternates 2.2:1, 4:5 and 3:2 preview compositions on desktop; mobile landscape technical previews retain 16:9. On explicit full playback the ungraded YouTube player uses original 16:9 framing. The treatment does not alter source files, transcript timestamps or standalone film pages. No grain animation or secondary motion is added.
