Using **OpenStax (candidate A)**, which states that a perfectly elastic 1D collision conserves both momentum and kinetic energy ([OpenStax](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics/pages/8-4-elastic-collisions-in-one-dimension)):

\[
3(2)+2(-1)=3v_1'+2v_2'
\]

\[
\tfrac12(3)(2^2)+\tfrac12(2)(-1)^2
=\tfrac12(3)v_1'^2+\tfrac12(2)v_2'^2
\]

Solving these equations gives:

- **Object 1:** \(v_1'=-0.4\ \text{m/s}\)
- **Object 2:** \(v_2'=2.6\ \text{m/s}\)

Check: total momentum remains \(4\ \text{kg·m/s}\), and total kinetic energy remains \(7\ \text{J}\).
