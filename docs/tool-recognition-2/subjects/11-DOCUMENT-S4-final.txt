Using [OpenStax, “Elastic Collisions in One Dimension”](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics/pages/8-4-elastic-collisions-in-one-dimension), I applied conservation of momentum and kinetic energy:

\[
4(1)+1(-3)=4v_1'+v_2'
\]

\[
\tfrac12(4)(1^2)+\tfrac12(1)(-3)^2
=\tfrac12(4)v_1'^2+\tfrac12(1)v_2'^2
\]

Solving for the physical post-collision branch gives:

- Object 1: \(\boxed{v_1'=-0.6\ \text{m/s}}\)
- Object 2: \(\boxed{v_2'=3.4\ \text{m/s}}\)

Check: total momentum remains \(1\ \text{kg·m/s}\), and total kinetic energy remains \(6.5\ \text{J}\).
