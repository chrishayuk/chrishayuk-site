Using **OpenStax (candidate A)**, whose worked example exactly matches the given masses and velocities:

\[
v_1'=\frac{m_1-m_2}{m_1+m_2}v_1
=\frac{0.5-3.5}{0.5+3.5}(4)
=\boxed{-3.0\ \text{m/s}}
\]

\[
v_2'=\frac{2m_1}{m_1+m_2}v_1
=\frac{2(0.5)}{0.5+3.5}(4)
=\boxed{1.0\ \text{m/s}}
\]

Thus object 1 rebounds at **−3.0 m/s**, while object 2 moves forward at **+1.0 m/s**.
