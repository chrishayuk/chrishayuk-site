Using [OpenStax, candidate A](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics/pages/8-4-elastic-collisions-in-one-dimension), I applied conservation of momentum and kinetic energy:

\[
2(3)+1(-1)=2v'_1+v'_2
\]

\[
\tfrac12(2)(3^2)+\tfrac12(1)(-1)^2
=\tfrac12(2)v_1'^2+\tfrac12(1)v_2'^2
\]

Solving for the nontrivial collision outcome gives:

- **Object 1:** \(v'_1=\frac13\ \text{m/s}\approx0.333\ \text{m/s}\)
- **Object 2:** \(v'_2=\frac{13}{3}\ \text{m/s}\approx4.333\ \text{m/s}\)

Checks: total momentum remains \(5\ \text{kg·m/s}\), and total kinetic energy remains \(9.5\ \text{J}\).
