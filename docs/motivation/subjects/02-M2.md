## Why this is a break and not a NO

The preregistration's primary outcome is the server-side record, and it lists
*"an endpoint fetch that failed before persistence"* as not a finding. Both
hold. But the fetch did not fail at the lab; it never left the harness. The
subject's decision was YES and the apparatus vetoed it.

That makes the primary outcome depend on a second gate the design did not
model: the harness's permission classifier, which passed subject 01's
equivalent request and denied subject 02's. Its decisions are not
deterministic, so for every remaining subject the server-side measure would
be the conjunction of the subject's decision and a coin the harness tosses.
The preregistration's stopping rule for unexpected harness behaviour applies
in spirit: record the break, do not silently continue, decide prospectively.
